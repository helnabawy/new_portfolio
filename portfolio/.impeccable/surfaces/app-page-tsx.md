# Surface: home page (app/page.tsx)

Scope: the single portfolio page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: recruiters and hiring managers confirming seniority, scale and stack fit in about a minute. Action: email Hosam, or download the CV. Proof: CV figures only (see PRODUCT.md). Constraints: static export, configurable base path, no screenshots or portrait, nothing fabricated.

Chosen direction: Store Listing (the user picked it on the decision page over the rolled Component Datasheet). Memorable moment: the preview gallery, where the slots an app listing gives to screenshots hold animated infographics of the CV's real figures.

Unresolved: real store URLs for the projects; whether the CV PDF (which includes a phone number) should stay downloadable.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Hosam is presented the way his own work reaches people, as an app store product page. It refuses the dark developer portfolio of centred hero, glow and equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, grouped grey #f2f2f7 fields, near-black ink, one store blue #0a66ff for every action. Squircle icons in flat colour, pill buttons, a divided fact strip, tall rounded preview panels as full colour fields, hairline-ruled information rows. One grotesk in heavy and regular weights with tabular numerals.

STORY: The visitor sees who he is and the scale of his work, watches the figures demonstrated in the previews, reads the career as version history, checks the stack, then contacts him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred 1040px column. Blue squircle monogram 210px at left; beside it the name at 64px, title, and a blue Contact pill with Download CV next to it. Below, a five-cell fact strip between hairlines. Below that, the top of the preview panel row, the first panel blue.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: a draggable, snap-scrolling preview gallery whose panels each animate their figure when they enter view; a sticky bar that gains the mini icon, name and Contact pill once the header scrolls away. Motion grammar: springs and exponential ease-out, count-ups, bars and dots that draw from a visible default.

FORM: Store Listing, my top-ranked grounded candidate of seven; rolled assignment was candidate 5 (Component Datasheet). Seed key b5601a17.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
